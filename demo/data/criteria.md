# Violation criteria by checklist item

What counts as D1, D2, or D3 for each item in the DEMO checklist. This document is illustrative only and does not describe any real audit methodology.

## DEM01

D1:
- One or two bin lids are left open but the bin is not overflowing
- Trash is visible at the rim of an otherwise closed bin

D2:
- Bins are overflowing with trash visible on the ground around them
- Bin lids are missing or broken so the bin cannot be closed at all

## DEM02

D1:
- Light dust or fingerprints on the door glass or handle
- Minor smudges that do not obscure visibility through the glass

## DEM03

D1:
- Crumbs, spills, or light dust left on a table or chair after cleaning
- A single chair with a loose leg or minor cosmetic scratch

D2:
- Sticky residue or food stains left on multiple tables at the same time
- A chair or table that is structurally unstable and unsafe to use

## DEM04

D2:
- A menu board still shows an item or price that was discontinued or changed
- A promotional display references an offer that has already ended

## DEM05

D1:
- A product is within one day of its shelf-life expiry but has not yet been used
- The date label on a product is smudged or hard to read, though the product is still within date

D2:
- A product past its shelf-life date is found in a storage or prep area, not yet used
- Staff cannot say when an unlabeled product was prepared or opened

D3:
- An expired product is found already in use in an order being prepared
- An expired product is served or leaves the kitchen as part of a finished order

## DEM06

D1:
- A surface is visibly clean but was not sanitized between two similar food types
- Sanitizing wipes or spray are present but the last sanitizing pass was missed for one board

D2:
- A cutting board is used for raw and ready-to-eat product without sanitizing in between
- Visible food residue from a previous product remains on a working surface in active use

## DEM07

D1:
- Goods are stored slightly less than 15 cm off the floor but stacked safely
- A shelf is placed close to a wall but airflow is not fully blocked

D2:
- Goods are stored directly on the floor
- Boxes are stacked against a wall in a way that blocks cleaning and airflow entirely

## DEM08

D1:
- An opened container has a date label that is faded or partly illegible
- One opened container out of several is missing its date label

## DEM09

D1:
- A uniform has a minor stain or wrinkle but is otherwise worn correctly
- A name badge or hat is missing for one staff member during a short shift

D2:
- A uniform is visibly dirty or torn and still worn during service
- Required protective clothing (apron, hairnet) is not worn at all during food handling

## DEM10

D1:
- The soap dispenser is low but not empty
- Paper towels are available but the dispenser is nearly out

D2:
- The handwashing sink has no soap, no paper towels, or no hot water at all
- The handwashing sink is blocked by equipment or storage and cannot be used
